Gutter cleaning & installation in Washington Avenue Coalition and Memorial Park

Houston’s Super Neighborhood 22 extends from downtown and Buffalo Bayou west to I-610, with northern boundaries that include Hempstead Road, rail tracks, White Oak Bayou, and I-10.

The City profile covers several neighborhoods and major parkland, so a gutter proposal must identify the exact building and elevations.

A large area with different property contexts

The City describes Memorial Park, Camp Logan, and Crestwood on the western side, and Old Sixth Ward and First Ward on the eastern side. It also notes more than ten neighborhoods and a mix of residential and changing commercial areas. That broad range is why a locality page cannot substitute for an address-specific scope. A detached house, apartment building, or commercial property can have different roof access, gutter lengths, and discharge routes. Ask the provider to list the property sections and components being serviced, with any inaccessible or excluded elevations stated clearly.

Keep landmarks in context

Memorial Park and the Houston Arboretum are important features identified by the City, along with Buffalo Bayou and White Oak Bayou as boundary references. These landmarks help orient the super neighborhood; they do not prove that an individual building borders a park or bayou, or that it has a specific drainage condition. If a property is near trees or open space, describe the actual debris seen on its roof or in its gutters. A service recommendation should be grounded in those observations rather than assumptions about the whole neighborhood.

Match the service to the building

For routine cleaning, define the runs and outlets to be cleared and whether debris removal is included. For repair, identify the leaking seam, loose support, blocked outlet, or other observed concern. For installation or replacement, measure the roof area, pitch, valleys, outlet layout, downspouts, and discharge route. A larger gutter is not automatically appropriate because of a park or bayou nearby. The design should be explained with respect to the property. Guards may limit some debris entry but still require inspection and maintenance.

Coordinate service around occupied spaces

The super neighborhood includes residential areas, commercial sites, historic districts, parks, and transportation corridors. Work planning should account for the individual property’s access and use: entries, sidewalks, parking, tenant schedules, and deliveries. A proposal should say whether removal and cleanup are included and what happens if concealed fascia damage is discovered. Roof repair, structural work, grading, and underground drainage are separate scopes unless expressly listed. No gutter installation can guarantee that a site will avoid flooding in extreme weather or downstream drainage conditions.

Check property-specific historic considerations

Houston identifies Old Sixth Ward and First Ward at the eastern edge of this super neighborhood and says they were the City’s first Protected Historic Districts. The larger SN 22 boundary also includes many other areas, so the designation should never be assumed for every property in the page’s service geography. If an address may be in a protected district, confirm applicable review requirements before selecting a visible gutter profile or color. The installer can provide a written description of materials, placement, and attachment approach to support that review. Functional sizing still depends on the roof and drainage route, not on historic designation. at this address.

Questions about this service

What boundaries define Super Neighborhood 22?

The City lists downtown on the east, Buffalo Bayou on the south, I-610 on the west, and Hempstead Road, rail tracks, White Oak Bayou, and I-10 on the north.

Does Memorial Park mean a property is in the park?

No. The park is within the broader super-neighborhood area. Check the exact address and boundary before making a property-level claim.

Can tree cover alone determine cleaning frequency?

No. Use actual debris buildup, roof configuration, and observed flow to choose a practical maintenance interval.
